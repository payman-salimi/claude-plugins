# Joint Angle Standards — Exercise Form Reference

Biomechanical reference values used by the form-correction skill. All angles in degrees, measured from 2D normalized landmarks (x, y plane). Z depth is noted where relevant but treated as supporting signal only.

---

## How angles are defined

All angles are computed at the **vertex joint** using the three-point formula:

```
angle_at_B = arccos( (BA · BC) / (|BA| × |BC|) )
```

Where A-B-C are three landmarks with B at the vertex. Angles are always 0–180°.

**Spine lean** is measured as the angle between the mid-shoulder–mid-hip line and the vertical axis (0° = perfectly upright, 90° = horizontal).

## Squat variants

| Measurement | Back squat (low bar) | Back squat (high bar) | Front squat | Goblet squat | Box squat |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Min knee angle at depth | 80–100° | 85–95° | 85–95° | 90–100° | 90–110° (box height dependent) |
| Trunk forward lean | 40–55° | 30–45° | 15–30° | 15–25° | 30–50° |
| Hip angle at depth | 85–110° | 90–105° | 100–120° | 105–120° | 90–110° |
| Knee tracking | Neutral or slight external rotation over 2nd toe | Same | Same | Same | Same |

**Asymmetry tolerance**: L/R knee angle delta ≤ 8° is acceptable. 9–14° = watch and monitor. ≥ 15° = flag for lateral imbalance or mobility restriction.

**Depth benchmark**:
- Parallel = hip crease at knee level → approximately knee angle of 90°
- ATG (ass-to-grass) = knee angle ≤ 70°
- Quarter squat = knee angle ≥ 130°

## Deadlift variants

### Conventional

| Measurement | Setup (bar over mid-foot) | Mid-pull | Lockout |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hip angle | 45–65° | Increases progressively | 170–180° |
| Knee angle | 65–85° | Increases progressively | 170–180° |
| Spine lean from vertical | 40–55° | Decreases as bar rises | 0–10° |
| Hip rise rate | Hip and bar rise at equal rate — no early hip rise | — | — |

**Early hip rise flag**: If hip angle increases by > 15° in the first 20% of the pull while knee angle increases < 5°, hips are rising too fast.

### Sumo

| Measurement | Setup | Lockout |
|---|---|---|
| Hip angle | 30–55° | 170–180° |
| Knee angle | 55–80° | 170–180° |
| Stance width | Feet outside shoulder width — landmark confirmation: ankle landmarks wider than shoulder landmarks | — |
| Trunk lean | 20–40° (more upright than conventional) | 0–10° |

### Romanian / Stiff-leg

| Measurement | Bottom (max hip hinge) | Start / lockout |
|---|---|---|
| Hip angle | 30–55° | 170–175° |
| Knee angle | Soft bend only: 150–165° | 165–175° |
| Spine lean | 50–70° (hip dominant, hamstring stretch) | 0–10° |

## Hip thrust / Glute bridge

| Measurement | Bottom | Top (lockout) |
|---|---|---|
| Hip angle | 90–110° (supine, knees bent) | 170–180° (full hip extension) |
| Knee angle | 80–100° (feet flat) | 80–100° (unchanged) |
| Spine neutral | Thoracic at bench, no lumbar overextension at top | Same |

**Hyperextension flag**: Hip angle > 185° at top OR visible lumbar extension in screenshot = over-thrusting, compensating with lower back.

---

## Bench press

| Measurement | Bottom (touch) | Lockout |
|---|---|---|
| Elbow angle | 75–95° | 165–175° |
| Shoulder angle (elbow-shoulder-hip) | 45–75° (grip-width dependent) | 85–100° |
| Wrist-to-elbow vertical alignment | Wrist directly above elbow (within 5 cm real-world) | Same |

**Elbow flare warning**: Shoulder angle > 85° at bottom = high-elbow position, elevated shoulder impingement risk. Recommend 45–60° tuck.

**Powerlifting arch vs. standard**: Large arch acceptable if intentional. Flag if spine leaves bench > 15 cm estimated and client has not declared powerlifting context.

---

## Overhead press (standing)

| Measurement | Rack position | Lockout |
|---|---|---|
| Elbow angle | 80–95° | 165–175° |
| Shoulder angle | 70–90° (elbows slightly in front of bar) | Arms by ears — shoulder/wrist x-coordinates aligned |
| Trunk lean | 0–10° backward lean acceptable at lockout | Same |

**Lumbar extension warning**: Spine lean > 20° backward at lockout = using lumbar hyperextension to complete the rep. Rib flare + forward hip = the signature.

---

## Pull-up / Chin-up

| Measurement | Dead hang | Top of pull |
|---|---|---|
| Elbow angle | 170–180° | 55–75° (full ROM pull) |
| Shoulder angle | 180° overhead | 65–85° |
| Trunk angle consistency | < 10° variance between reps = controlled; > 20° = kipping / momentum |

**Partial rep flag**: Elbow angle at top > 100° = not achieving a full range pull.

## Row variants

### Barbell row (bent over)

| Measurement | Start | Top (elbow at 90°) |
|---|---|---|
| Hip angle | 30–50° (hip hinge, parallel-ish torso) | Same |
| Elbow angle | 165–175° | 75–95° |
| Spine lean | 40–55° from vertical | Same (should not change during the rep) |

**Spine movement flag**: Spine lean that increases > 10° during the pull = using momentum / body English rather than lat/rhomboid strength.

### Cable / dumbbell row

| Measurement | Extended | Contracted |
|---|---|---|
| Elbow angle | 165–175° | 75–95° |
| Shoulder retraction | Visible in screenshot — blade should retract at contraction | — |

## Lunge variants

| Measurement | Front lunge (bottom) | Reverse lunge (bottom) | Split squat (bottom) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Front knee angle | 85–100° | 85–100° | 85–95° |
| Rear knee angle | 85–100° (ideally just off floor) | 85–100° | 90–100° |
| Trunk lean | 5–15° forward | 5–15° forward | 5–15° forward |
| Front knee tracking | Knee over 2nd toe — no valgus | Same | Same |

**Knee over toe flag**: Front knee x-coordinate moves medially past the ankle x-coordinate = valgus collapse. Corrective: glute/hip strengthening + cue.

---

## Asymmetry reference

| Joint pair | Acceptable delta | Flag threshold | Likely cause |
|---|---|---|---|
| L/R knee | ≤ 8° | ≥ 15° | Hip mobility, glute weakness, ankle restriction |
| L/R hip | ≤ 8° | ≥ 12° | SI joint, hip flexor tightness, lateral strength deficit |
| L/R shoulder | ≤ 10° | ≥ 15° | Dominant side compensation, rotator cuff asymmetry |
| L/R elbow (pressing) | ≤ 8° | ≥ 12° | Wrist/shoulder mobility, dominant side overuse |

Asymmetry ≥ flag threshold = note it, don't alarm. Recommend single-leg / unilateral work and mobility assessment. Refer to PT if delta > 20° and client has pain on the weaker side.

## Camera angle quality notes

| Camera position | Angles available | Quality |
|---|---|---|
| True side (90° from athlete) | Spine lean, hip angle, knee angle, ankle dorsiflexion | Best |
| Front-facing | Knee valgus/varus, shoulder symmetry, lateral shift | Best for asymmetry |
| Diagonal (45°) | Partial read on all — use with caution | Acceptable |
| Behind athlete | Knee tracking, hip width — spine and hip angle unreliable | Poor for most |
| Overhead | Not useful for standard lifting | Avoid |

When side view is available: spine and sagittal plane angles are reliable.
When front view is available: valgus, varus, asymmetry are reliable. Sagittal angles (spine lean, hip angle) are unreliable.
